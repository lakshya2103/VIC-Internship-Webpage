How This Internship Works

A flexible pathway from tasks to certificate

Choose an internship pathway, complete the required tasks and Capstone Assignment, use the supporting learning resources, and earn a certificate based on your completed work.

01

→

Complete internship tasks

→

→

About this internship

This internship, delivered virtually, aims to simulate a workplace-like experience for the learner. Learners are exposed to a set of real tasks that together form an Internship pathway. Each task trains one core practical skill.

How it works

  • There are 12 tasks. Each one builds a different skill.
  • You choose your tasks. Pick the tasks that interest you and complete them in any order.
  • Your certificate depends on how many tasks you complete.

Why the tasks

These activities collectively provide evidence for the attainment of the National Board of Accreditation / Washington Accord Graduate Attributes and support the achievement of Program Outcomes (PO1-PO12). Through a combination of internships, research projects, innovation activities, community engagement, professional development programs, and experiential learning opportunities, interns develop the technical, professional, societal, and lifelong learning competencies expected of STEM learners.

1. Engineering Knowledge (PO1)

The intern shall demonstrate the application of science & engineering fundamentals, mathematical concepts, and domain-specific knowledge through participation in industrial internships, technical projects, and practical assignments. Such experiences provide evidence of the ability to translate theoretical learning into professional applications.

2. Problem Analysis (PO2)

The intern shall engage in research, consultancy, or industry-oriented projects requiring the identification, formulation, and analysis of real world problems. This activity develops the capability to apply analytical methods and scientific judgment to complex problem-solving situations.

3. Design and Development of Solutions (PO3)

The intern shall participate in innovation and entrepreneurship activities, design competitions, hackathons, and similar initiatives that require the development of feasible and effective solutions. These experiences demonstrate the ability to design systems, components, or processes to meet specified needs while considering practical constraints.

4. Investigation of Complex Problems (PO4)

The intern shall undertake project work, industrial training, research assignments, or internship experiences involving experimentation, data collection, analysis, and interpretation. Such activities provide evidence of competence in conducting investigations and arriving at evidence-based conclusions.

5. Modern Tool Usage (PO5)

The intern shall gain proficiency in the use of contemporary software, simulation platforms, modeling tools, and digital technologies relevant to the discipline. This demonstrates the ability to select and apply modern tools effectively in professional practice.

6. The Intern and Society (PO6)

The intern shall participate in community engagement, rural development initiatives, or societal-impact projects. These activities promote an understanding of the societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural dimensions of practice.

7. Environment and Sustainability (PO7)

The intern shall contribute to sustainability-focused projects, environmental initiatives, or community-service activities that address ecological challenges. Such participation demonstrates awareness of sustainable development principles and environmental responsibilities associated with scientific decisions.

8. Ethics (PO8)

The intern shall exhibit professional integrity, accountability, honesty, punctuality, and adherence to institutional and organizational codes of conduct during academic and professional engagements. This demonstrates commitment to ethical principles and responsible professional practice.

9. Individual and Team Work (PO9)

The intern shall actively contribute to the planning and execution of conferences, workshops, competitions, intern chapters, or multidisciplinary projects. Such involvement develops the ability to function effectively both as an individual and as a member or leader of diverse teams.

10. Communication (PO10)

The intern shall participate in industrial training, communication-development programs, technical presentations, and professional interactions. These experiences strengthen the ability to communicate technical information effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences.

11. Project Management and Finance (PO11)

The intern shall gain exposure to project planning, resource allocation, budgeting processes, and organizational decision-making through participation in project discussions and management-related activities. This develops an understanding of STEM management principles and financial considerations in project execution.

12. Life-Long Learning (PO12)

The intern shall demonstrate a commitment to continuous learning through self-directed skill development, application of acquired knowledge, innovation, and professional growth activities. This reflects the ability to adapt to emerging technologies and evolving professional requirements throughout a career.
